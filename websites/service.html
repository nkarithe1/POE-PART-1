What we build

Four services, all built by the same three benches in Linden.

Four services we provide

Custom furniture

Dining tables, benches, bed frames, sideboards and case pieces, designed to your room's dimensions and built in a solid wood species of your choice — typically Oregon pine, blackwood, oak or American ash. Joinery is cut by hand wherever the design allows it, rather than relying on screws or dowels.

From R 8,500 · 4–8 week lead time · Delivered & installed, Gauteng

Fitted joinery & shopfitting

Built-in wardrobes, kitchen cabinetry, home libraries and retail fit-outs such as counters and shelving. We measure the space ourselves before drawing anything, so fitted pieces are built to fit — not adjusted on installation day.

From R 14,000 · 6–10 week lead time · Site visit included

Restoration & re-finishing

Re-gluing loose joints, replacing damaged veneer or timber, and hand-finishing with oil, wax or shellac to match a piece's original character. We assess every restoration in person before quoting, since structural condition varies far more than a photo can show.

From R 1,800 · 1–3 week lead time · Free assessment

Small commissions & gifts

Cutting boards, stools, shelving and one-off gift pieces — a lower-cost way to work with the workshop, or to try a wood species before committing to a larger piece.

From R 650 · 1–2 week lead time · Local collection or courier
