Thembisa and Son Woodworks

A five-person workshop making solid-wood furniture, fitted joinery and considered restorations for homes and small businesses across Johannesburg. No veneer, no shortcuts — every piece is designed, cut and finished on site.

Three ways to work with us

Most clients arrive with one of three needs: a single piece, a fitted space, or an old piece worth saving. We keep our workshop small on purpose, so the person who draws your piece is the person who builds it.

Tables, benches & case pieces

Designed to your dimensions, in a wood species you choose, built using traditional joinery rather than fasteners wherever the piece allows it.

Built-in storage & shopfitting

Cabinetry, shelving and counters fitted to an existing room or retail space, measured and installed by the same team that builds it.

Repairs & re-finishing

Structural repairs, re-gluing loose joints, and hand-finishing for furniture that's worth keeping rather than replacing.

From sketch to delivery

How a commission runs, from first message to installed piece.

1. Enquiry

Tell us the piece, the space and the budget. We reply within two working days.

2. Drawing & quote

A measured drawing and a fixed quote, so there are no surprises once cutting starts.

3. Build

Your piece is built on our Linden bench. Most commissions take four to eight weeks.

4. Delivery

We deliver and install within Gauteng, and crate for courier further afield.
