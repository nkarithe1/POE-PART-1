Small on purpose

Thembisa and Son started as a corner shop, where the Thembisa family set out to build a legacy producing well-crafted furniture for the people of Gauteng.

How we got here

Thabo Thembisa trained as a carpenter in Pretoria before spending six years restoring furniture for antique dealers in Johannesburg. In 2014 he took a lease on a corner workshop in Linden with one workbench and a set of hand tools inherited from his own teacher.

The business grew by referral, not advertising: a restored church pew led to a dining table, and a dining table led to a fitted kitchen. By 2019 the workshop had outgrown the original corner and moved to its current premises on Kloof Road, where the whole team now works on one open floor.

Milestones

  • 2014 — First workshop: a single bench rented in Linden.
  • 2017 — First apprentice: Naledi Mokwena joins the workshop.
  • 2019 — Kloof Road workshop: the current, larger premises opens.
  • 2022 — Melville showroom: a small display space opens for finished pieces and consultations.
  • 2026 — 240th commission: a fitted library for a Parkhurst client, delivered this year.

Why we do it

To build furniture and fitted joinery designed for the specific person and room it's made for, using methods and materials that mean the piece is still worth owning in thirty years.

Where we're headed

To stay a workshop, not a factory — growing our skill and our client base without growing past the point where every piece still passes across the same three benches.

Who we build for

Mostly Johannesburg homeowners commissioning a single piece or a fitted room. A smaller share are small businesses — cafés, studios and boutiques — needing counters, shelving or a shopfit that has to survive daily use.

Our team
