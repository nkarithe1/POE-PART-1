How we got here

thabo thembisa trained as a carpintar in Pretoria before spending six years restoring furniture
for antique dealers in Johannesburg. In 2014 he took a lease on a corner workshop in Linden
with one workbench and a set of hand tools inherited from his own teacher

The business grew by referral, not advertising: a restored church pew led to a dining table, and
a dining table led to a fitted kitchen. By 2019 Hearthstone had outgrown the original corner and
moved into its current workshop on Kloof Road, where the whole team now works on one open floor

Why we do what we do

To build furniture and fitted joinery that is designed for the specific person and room it's made for,
using joinery methods and materials that mean the piece is still worth owning in thirty years.

Where we're headed

To stay a workshop, not a factory — growing our skill and our client base without growing past
the point where every piece still passes across the same three benches.

who we build for

Homeowners, and the odd small business,Most of our clients are Johannesburg homeowners commissioning a
single piece or a fitted room. A smaller share are small businesses — cafés, studios and boutiques — needing
counters, shelving or a shopfit that has to survive daily use.
