            <h2>four services we provide</h2>
            <ul>
                <lo><h3>custom furniture</h3></lo>
                <p>Dining tables, benches, bed frames, sideboards and case pieces,<br>
                    designed to your room's dimensions and built in a solid wood species <br>
                    of your choice — typically Oregon pine, blackwood, oak or American ash.<br>
                     Joinery is cut by hand wherever the design allows it, rather than relying<br>
                      on screws or dowels<br>
                    From R 8,500 4–8 week lead time Delivered & installed, Gauteng</p>
                      <ol><h3>Fitted joinery & shopfitting</h3></ol>
                      <p>Built-in wardrobes, kitchen cabinetry, home libraries and retail fit-outs<br>
                         such as counters and shelving. We measure the space ourselves before drawing <br>
                         anything, so fitted pieces are built to fit — not adjusted on installation day.<br>
                         From R 14,000 6–10 week lead time Site visit included</p>
                         <ol><h3>Restoration & re-finishing</h3></ol>
                         <p>Re-gluing loose joints, replacing damaged veneer or timber, and hand-finishing<br>
                             with oil, wax or shellac to match a piece's original character. We assess every<br>
                              restoration in person before quoting, since structural condition varies far more<br>
                               than a photo can show.<br>
                             From R 1,800 1–3 week lead time Free assessment </p>
                             <ol><h3>Small commissions & gifts</h3></ol>
                             <p>Cutting boards, stools, shelving and one-off gift pieces — a lower-cost way to work<br>
                                 with the workshop, or to try a wood species before committing to a larger piece.<br>
                                From R 650 1–2 week lead time Local collection or courier </p>
            </ul>
